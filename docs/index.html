Does Geometric Unity stand up?

Geometric Unity (GU) is a draft theory by Eric Weinstein, from April 2021.

We check it in Lean. Lean is a proof checker. It accepts only airtight proofs.

02Finding

On floor 4, the arrow of time flips.

Before matter can live on the 14-dim bundle, GU needs one consistent arrow of time on it, everywhere.

Start: the arrow of time points one way.

  1. Pick a loop of metrics. (A metric is a way to measure distances.)
  2. Walk once around it, carrying the arrow of time.
  3. The arrow comes back reversed, like on a Möbius strip.

So GU’s spinors (matter) cannot exist on the full space, as written.

Machine-proved · Phase 4

What if GU means a two-piece group?

We checked this other reading too. Its second piece flips the sign of every spinor pairing (machine-proved). That this breaks GU’s matter setup is our reading, not yet machine-checked.

Sign flip machine-proved · Phase 6

03Fix

The fix: a double cover.

Take two sheets, glued together. One loop takes you to the other sheet. Two loops bring you home.

On the double cover, the arrow of time is consistent. Spinors exist on flat space, and on any spacetime with a global frame. In general: open.

Start: sheet A.

  • Arrow of time, for every spacetimeMachine-proved
  • Spinors on ℝ⁴ (flat 4D space)Machine-proved
  • Spinors, any spacetime with a global frameMachine-proved
  • Spinors in general, locallyYes
  • Spinors in general, globallyOpen question

The idea of a double cover is standard. Applying it here, and proving it by machine, is new as far as we found.
